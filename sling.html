Sling

Quick capture for iPhone. Type or dictate a note, swipe up, and Sling saves it as plain Markdown to iCloud Drive, a Files folder, or an automation webhook.

Support

Need help with Sling? Email sober@duck.com with your device model, iOS version, and what you were trying to do.

Common Questions

Where do my notes save?

By default, Sling saves notes to iCloud Drive. You can also choose any Files-compatible folder in Settings.

What happens if my destination is unavailable?

Sling tries to save the note locally in the Files app container so your note is not lost.

Do I need an account?

No. Sling does not have accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

How do I use webhook delivery?

Open Settings, tap Connect Webhook, and paste a Sling connection link. Sling previews the destination and verifies the link before saving it.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

This privacy policy describes how 47 Summers LLC ("we", "us") handles information in connection with Sling.

Summary

Sling is a quick-capture app for saving notes as plain Markdown files. Sling does not require an account, does not include ads, and does not include analytics or tracking SDKs.

What Sling Stores

  • App settings: Sling stores preferences on your device, such as sound, haptics, text size, selected destination, and clear-gesture settings.
  • Folder access: If you choose a custom folder, Sling stores a security-scoped folder bookmark so it can keep saving notes to that folder.
  • Webhook connection: If you connect a webhook, Sling stores the secret token in the iOS Keychain and stores non-secret connection details, such as the destination name and URL, in app settings.
  • Draft text: If you leave an unfinished note, Sling may store that draft locally so it can be restored when you reopen the app.

Notes And Destinations

By default, Sling saves notes to the app's iCloud Drive folder. iCloud syncing is handled by Apple and your Apple ID settings.

If you choose a folder through the Files picker, Sling saves notes to that folder. That folder may be local to your device or provided by another app or cloud storage service.

If you connect a webhook, Sling sends note content to the webhook URL you configured. The destination service may process or store that content according to its own policies. Sling does not operate the webhook destination unless you host it yourself.

If a configured destination is unavailable, Sling tries to save the note locally in the Files app container so the note is not lost.

Microphone And Speech Recognition

Sling asks for microphone and speech recognition permission only if you use voice dictation. Dictation is handled through Apple's system speech-recognition services. Sling does not record, sell, or use your voice for advertising.

What Sling Does Not Do

  • require an account
  • collect analytics
  • show ads
  • track you across apps or websites
  • sell personal data
  • upload your notes to a Sling-owned server

Deleting Your Data

You can delete saved notes from the folder where they were saved, including iCloud Drive, a custom folder, or the local Files app container.

You can disconnect a webhook in Settings. You can remove local Sling data by deleting the app from your device. Notes that were already saved to iCloud Drive or another folder may remain in those locations until you delete them there.
